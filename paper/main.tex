\begin{abstract}
Coding agents built a suite of \suitePackages{} benchmark packages holding \suiteTasks{} tasks on one shared engine, and probing the packages found defects that needed a fix round in every one of them (median \reviewRoundsMedian{}, maximum \reviewRoundsMax{}), across \defectClasses{} defect classes; at least \defectBlindStrategyCredit{} packages paid credit to a strategy that ignored the task. Re-running the logged exploits on the shipped artifacts replicates \expExplReplicated{} of \expExplRecordClauses{} record clauses, \expExplReplReleased{} of \expExplRecordReleased{} measured on the published tarballs and \expExplReplCommit{} of \expExplRecordCommit{} on the unreleased commits the record cited, and the median attack score over the \expExplAtkPairs{} paired wave-1 rows falls from \expExplAtkMedianOldPaired{} before the fixes to \expExplAtkMedianNewPaired{} after; \expExplFixFails{} of the \expExplPostfixClauses{} post-fix clauses still fail at the shipped versions. The other layers did not stay blind in both directions: the diff-reading review flagged a published hidden seed before one merge, and the generic blind battery later found released credit on \expTrivialReleasedMaxPkgs{} that the probing record never logged. Under the preregistered design the best of \trivialPolicies{} generic blind policies scored higher before the fixes in only \trivialHigherOld{} of \trivialPackages{} packages ($p = \trivialSignP{}$ on \trivialSignN{} non-tied packages), a null that bounds what the registered design can claim; at the released versions the best blind score on any package is \expTrivialReleasedMax{}. The released graders still break their own rules: \expProbesFailedCells{} of \expProbesReleasedCells{} released-version probe cells fail their frozen rule and \expProbesForgedAccepted{} of \expPackages{} graders accept forged fields. The record documents \approvalDefectCases{} of the \councilMergedApproved{} approvals standing behind defects a later probing review found. We release the engine, the packages, the probing protocol, the defect record and the scripts behind every number, and we condense the classes into five checks to run before an agent-built benchmark ships. No real model was run and no leaderboard is kept; the registered real-model arm is unfinished.
\end{abstract}
