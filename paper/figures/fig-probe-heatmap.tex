\caption{Contract probes at each package's released version, generated from the contract-probe outputs (Appendix~\ref{app:experiments}). Rows are the \expPackages{} packages (wave 1 at 0.2.0 above the dashed rule, wave 2 at its only 0.1.0 release below it), columns the \expProbes{} frozen probes: the \texttt{hv-} honest variants left of the first dashed rule, the single unpromised exploratory probe between the rules, and the \texttt{atk-} attacks right of it. A cell passes when the artifact's frozen expected rule held, fail cells carry a pale diagonal overlay so they stay distinct from pass cells in grayscale, and outside-contract or not-runnable cells are hatched rather than read as pass or fail. The orange \texttt{atk-dup-tool-calls} cells are expected penalties: those graders score a repeated side effect zero by design, so the margin bars count each row's genuine fails in black and stack the expected penalties on top in orange.}
