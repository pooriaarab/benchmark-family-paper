\section{Builders and AI disclosure}
\label{app:builders}

Table~\ref{tab:builders} counts the \texttt{Assisted-by} trailers on the \buildersPrs{} pull requests across the \suitePackages{} repositories, read from the request bodies on 2026-10-16 (\texttt{paper/data/builders.csv}, rebuilt by \texttt{scripts/builders.mjs}). Every request carries a trailer, so the suite was built mainly by coding agents, and \builderAgents{} agents share the work, led by \builderTopAgent{} (\builderTopAgentPrs{} of \buildersPrs{} pull requests).

The code and this draft were produced with AI coding agents under the author's direction and review, and the record is public. The council reviews are on the same record and are counted in Appendix~\ref{app:reviewstats}. The sessions that found the defects reported in Section~\ref{sec:review} also ran in coding-agent interfaces, configured by the author and handed a protocol; their model choice is not recorded in the repositories, so the paper does not name a reviewer model. The author is responsible for what shipped, including the decision to publish no model scores.
