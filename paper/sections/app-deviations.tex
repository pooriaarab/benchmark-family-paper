\section{Deviation log and free-model status}
\label{app:deviations}

The deviation log on the registered record keeps every change made before any real-model cell finished: one paid run was started and stopped within about two minutes, no cell finished, and the shared key's usage counter rose by at most 0.275 USD (D2); the roster moved from six paid models to five free ones (D3); the models run through a local proxy that refuses any model id not ending in \texttt{:free} (D4); the spending cap is moot at zero planned spend (D5); and one output cap and reasoning effort was set for every real-model request (D6). The runner also takes \texttt{-{}-concurrency}, \texttt{-{}-timeout}, and \texttt{-{}-retries} flags, which change timing and leave the tasks untouched, so they count as operational settings.

The grid runner and the analysis behind Section~\ref{sec:prereg} are \texttt{eval-matrix} and \texttt{eval-analyze} in \texttt{pooriaarab/scripts}, at commits \texttt{13dc1d6} and \texttt{3381492}.

The real-model arm runs the free-model roster of D3 through the local proxy of D4. The run was still in progress at the time of writing, and this paper reports no model numbers.
